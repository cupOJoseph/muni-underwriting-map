How to read this. The underwriters' discount is the spread a bank keeps when it buys a bond issue from the issuer and resells it to investors. Figures come from each deal's official statement, sale results or a state/agency debt report, linked in every row. "Medium" confidence means the fee was computed from the purchase price or taken from a secondary report. Negotiated deals usually cost more per $1,000 than competitive sales. Research compiled November 2026; this is informational, not investment advice.
